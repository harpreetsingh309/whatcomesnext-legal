Privacy Policy

What Comes Next? · Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile app What Comes Next? (“the App”) handles information. The App is designed for children and families and is built to work offline without requiring an account.

Information we collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location.

Gameplay progress (stars, coins, unlocked levels, stickers, settings) is stored locally on your device using Apple’s UserDefaults / on-device storage.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may share limited purchase entitlement information with the App so unlocked content can be restored.

Notifications

If you enable the optional daily reminder, the App schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder content is not sent to our servers.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites for advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for children with parental involvement for purchases and sensitive settings. Because progress stays on device and no account is required, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is needed for local gameplay.

Data retention and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until you reset progress in Settings, delete the App, or erase the device. Restoring purchases can re-unlock paid content after reinstall.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub or see our Support page.
