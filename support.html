Common questions

How do purchases work?

Premium unlocks all worlds, or you can buy individual world packs. Purchases use your Apple ID. Kids must ask a parent to complete the adult math gate before buying or restoring.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases (parents gate required), or use Restore on the unlock screen. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on this device only. Deleting the App removes local progress. Paid unlocks can be restored after reinstall.

Daily reminder

Optional daily reminders are local notifications. Turn them on or off in Settings. If reminders do not appear, check iOS Settings → Notifications for the App.
